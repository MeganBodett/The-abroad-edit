02 / The practical edit

Booked the flight. Now what?

All the things worth sorting before departure, so you can spend less time stressing and more time actually being there.

The suitcase situation

Pack for your real life.

  • Comfortable walking shoes and versatile layers.
  • Weather-appropriate clothes for your host city and weekend trips.
  • Chargers, plug adapters, medications, and essential toiletries.
  • Copies of important documents stored securely.

Check airline baggage limits and your housing’s laundry setup before you pack.

Documents & logistics

Things to double-check.

  • Passport validity and any required visa or residence paperwork.
  • Program requirements, health coverage, and emergency contacts.
  • Phone connectivity, bank travel settings, and payment backups.
  • Arrival transport and your first-night address.

Rules vary by nationality and destination. Confirm requirements with your program and official government sources.

Safety, always

Have fun. Have a plan.

  • Save local emergency numbers and your program’s emergency contact.
  • Share your travel plans with someone you trust.
  • Stay aware of belongings in crowded transit areas.
  • Plan a safe way home, especially late at night.

Travel advice here is general. Check official destination advisories and university guidance for current safety information.
